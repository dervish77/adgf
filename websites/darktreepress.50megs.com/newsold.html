02/14/2008

Hello, just a quick update to mention that the old email address that I've been using of editorNO@SPAMdarktreepress.com has been replaced with darktreepressNO@SPAMyahoo.com. The old address relied on a forwarding facility provided by my website hosting company, but it has been getting hammered by email spammers for the last 6 months or more, and apparently Yahoo's spam filter doesn't do a particularly good job when you are getting forwarded email. So I've decided to no longer use the forwarding feature and will now use a dedicated email address directly for all email communication. If this doesn't help curb the tide of spam, I will be forced to switch email services away from Yahoo. So as of today, the email forwarding will be disabled and you will need to use the new email address as noted above and on the "about" page. Sorry for any inconvenience.
