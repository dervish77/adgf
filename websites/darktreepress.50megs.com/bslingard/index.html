Welcome to my little corner in cyberspace.

Though currently living in Massachusetts, I consider myself as a native of New Hampshire, having grown up there. I am married to a wonderful woman and have two beautiful sons.

Professionally, I am an embedded software engineer. I received both a BS degree and an MS degree in electrical engineering from Worcester Polytechnic Institute (WPI). A few years ago, I formed Whirling Dervish Software to distribute free software and shareware. Programmers and computer graphics students may find something useful on that site. You can also find a text adventure game that I wrote called "Dungeons of Thuria" as a zip file on the Interactive Fiction Archive.

I'm a huge science fiction, comic book, and H.P. Lovecraft fan. I also enjoy writing stories and some poetry. I also enjoy tinkering with old computers and electronics projects. And for several years I have been researching my family history.

Years of collecting books, magazines, comics, toys and other things have left me with little room. Hence, I am selling a portion of my collection. I have started listing items on Ebay for auction.

While you're here, please sign my guestbook or read what others had to say.
