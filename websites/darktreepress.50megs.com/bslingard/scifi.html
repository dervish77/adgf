I started reading science fiction and fantasy books when I was still in grade school. The first real fantasy book I ever read was L'Engle's "A Wrinkle in Time". I remember reading Arthur C. Clarke's "2001" when I was just 12 years old. I devoured everything I could get my hands on. This page will highlight my favorite authors and books. I will also include some commentary about the current state of the genre.

The Star Trek Log

My first fanzine! Created for 6th
grade english class (3/1977). Based on
and inspired by Starlog Magazine. Had
14 typewritten pages. Cover was hand
drawn on heavy construction paper.
Contents included Shatner bio, fiction,
puzzles, and games.
