 Welcome to the author site for Brian S. Lingard, writer, editor, and publisher.

Brian writes mostly science fiction and horror short fiction, and occasionally some dark fantasy. Brian has been writing off and on since he was 14 years old. An avid reader and collector, Brian decided to give something back to the genre by starting Dark Tree Press, a small press publishing company specializing in H. P. Lovecraft, and science fiction/horror.
