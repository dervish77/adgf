The magazine of startling science fiction and weird horror!

Black Satellite (BS) is a digest sized print magazine publishing startling science fiction and weird horror. BS publishes original fiction and original artwork, on the covers and as interior illustrations.

Upcoming issues will feature stories by Albert J. Manachino, Lee Clark Zumpe, Tim Curran, Bill Wilson, Tom Olbert, Darrell Pitt and J. R. Cain.

Issues #1 through #3, #5 through #7 are available now. Issue #4 is now sold out. Issue #8 will appear late 2006. Issues #1 through #3 are nearly gone (See Below)!

Please see the ordering page for details on obtaining back issues or for subscription info. Also available via Project Pulp, on Shocklines.com, and at Mythos Books

Please query if you have something to submit or read our submission guidelines.
