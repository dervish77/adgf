Company Background

Whirling Dervish Software was founded in early 1997 as a vehicle for distributing various software applications written during graduate studies at Worcester Polytechnic Institute. Several of these programs were first developed during 1995, then later expanded upon and enhanced. Other programs were developed solely for educational and/or demonstration purposes. It is hoped that other programmers may benefit from a few of these programs during their studies.

Whirling Dervish Software specializes in computer gaming development, graphical user interface design, and multiplayer network gaming. The company also has experience in developing software for embedded systems such as diagnostics, device drivers, and low level protocols.

Currently in development is a multiplayer game that is simple to learn and fun to play for 2 to 16 people. Details will be forthcoming sometime later this year. The game will most likely be released as freeware, with target platforms to include both UNIX workstations and PC's running Windows 95.

Also in development is a new text adventure based upon H.P. Lovecraft's stories. The game will be implemented using one of the freely available text adventure parser languages such as Inform or TADS. Thus it will be playable on any system supporting a runtime parser engine for that system.
